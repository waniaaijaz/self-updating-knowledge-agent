# Acme People Policy 2026

## 1 Working Arrangements

### 1.1 Office Hours
Core office hours at Acme are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Meetings should be scheduled inside these hours where possible.

### 1.2 Remote Work
Acme staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week with the agreement of their team.

### 1.3 Annual Bonus
<!-- roles: hr -->
Acme staff are eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 8% of base salary, paid each March.

## 2 Leave

### 2.1 Annual Leave
Acme staff receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.

## 3 Management

### 3.1 Performance Reviews
<!-- roles: manager, hr -->
Acme managers complete a written performance review for each direct report twice per year, in June and December.

### 3.2 Hiring Approval
<!-- roles: manager, hr -->
Acme managers may hire up to 4 new staff per quarter without director approval.

## 4 Compensation

### 4.1 Salary Bands
<!-- roles: hr -->
Acme engineer salary bands range from 65,000 to 90,000 per year depending on level.

### 4.2 Disciplinary Records
<!-- roles: hr -->
Acme keeps disciplinary records on file for 3 years after the incident is closed.
