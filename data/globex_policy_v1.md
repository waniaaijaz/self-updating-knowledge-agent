# Globex People Policy 2024

## 1 Working Arrangements

### 1.1 Office Hours
Globex core hours run from 09:00 to 15:00 on weekdays, and the office closes at 19:00.

### 1.2 Remote Work
Globex staff are allowed to work from home 1 day per week.

## 2 Leave

### 2.1 Annual Leave
Globex staff receive 20 days of paid holiday per year, and up to 5 unused days may be carried over.

## 3 Management

### 3.1 Team Expenses
<!-- roles: manager, hr -->
Globex team leads may approve team expenses of up to 1,500 per month without finance sign-off.

### 3.2 Grievance Escalation
<!-- roles: manager -->
Globex team leads must escalate an unresolved grievance to the regional director within 5 working days.

## 4 Compensation

### 4.1 Salary Bands
<!-- roles: hr -->
Globex analyst salary bands range from 50,000 to 70,000 per year depending on grade.

### 4.2 Medical Leave Records
<!-- roles: hr -->
Globex stores medical leave certificates in the HR system for 2 years and never in team folders.
