# HR Policy Handbook 2024

## 1 Scope and Applicability

### 1.1 Coverage
This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Northwind Systems, including contractors engaged for more than 90 consecutive days.

### 1.2 Revision Cycle
This handbook is reviewed annually by the People Operations team. The version in effect is the most recently published edition.

## 2 Working Arrangements

### 2.1 Remote Work Eligibility
All engineering and product staff are permitted to work remotely up to 5 days per week. No in-office attendance is required.

### 2.2 Core Hours
Employees are expected to be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 in their local timezone. Outside core hours, scheduling is at the employee's discretion.

### 2.3 Workspace Registration
Employees working remotely must register their primary work location once at the time of onboarding. Re-registration is not required unless the country of residence changes.

## 3 Compensation and Allowances

### 3.1 Home Office Stipend
Remote employees receive an annual home office stipend of $500. The stipend is paid in a single instalment each January and does not require receipts.

### 3.2 Internet Reimbursement
Northwind reimburses up to $40 per month toward home internet costs for employees working remotely three or more days per week.

### 3.3 Travel Advances
Travel advances of up to $1,000 may be requested for pre-approved business travel. Advances are settled within 30 days of trip completion.

## 4 Leave

### 4.1 Paid Time Off
Employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. Unused PTO may be carried over into the following year without limit.

### 4.2 Leave Approval
PTO requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Requests submitted at least 5 business days in advance are approved automatically unless the manager objects.

### 4.3 Sick Leave
Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a medical certificate for absences of 3 days or fewer.

## 5 Equipment

### 5.1 Laptop Refresh Cycle
Company laptops are replaced every 4 years. Employees may keep the retired device.

### 5.2 Peripherals
Monitors, keyboards and headsets are ordered through the internal IT portal and do not count against the home office stipend.
