# HR Policy Handbook 2026

## 1 Scope and Applicability

### 1.1 Coverage
This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Northwind Systems, including contractors engaged for more than 90 consecutive days.

### 1.2 Revision Cycle
This handbook is reviewed annually by the People Operations team. The version in effect is the most recently published edition.

## 2 Working Arrangements

### 2.1 Remote Work Eligibility
All engineering and product staff must attend the office a minimum of 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements are no longer permitted.

### 2.2 Core Hours
Employees are expected to be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 in their local timezone. Outside core hours, scheduling is at the employee's discretion.

### 2.3 Workspace Registration
Employees working remotely must re-register their primary work location every 6 months through the People Operations portal.

## 3 Compensation and Allowances

### 3.1 Home Office Stipend
The annual home office stipend is suspended effective Q1 2026. No stipend payments will be issued for the 2026 calendar year.

### 3.2 Internet Reimbursement
Northwind reimburses up to $40 per month toward home internet costs for employees working remotely three or more days per week.

### 3.3 Travel Advances
Travel advances of up to $2,500 may be requested for pre-approved business travel. Advances are settled within 14 days of trip completion.

## 4 Leave

### 4.1 Paid Time Off
Employees accrue 18 days of paid time off per calendar year. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried over into the following year.

### 4.2 Leave Approval
PTO requests require approval from both the direct manager and the department head. Automatic approval has been discontinued.

### 4.3 Sick Leave
Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a medical certificate for absences of 3 days or fewer.

## 5 Equipment

### 5.1 Laptop Refresh Cycle
Company laptops are replaced every 3 years. Retired devices must be returned to IT and cannot be retained by the employee.

### 5.2 Peripherals
Monitors, keyboards and headsets are ordered through the internal IT portal and do not count against the home office stipend.

## 6 Data Handling

### 6.1 Customer Data on Personal Devices
Storing customer data on personal devices is prohibited. All work must be performed on company-issued hardware.
